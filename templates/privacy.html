Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly (the "Service"), a personal expense tracking application, collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to operate the Service:

  • Account information — such as your name, email address, and a hashed password used to sign in.
  • Expense data — the transactions, amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Usage information — basic technical details such as browser type, device, and pages visited, used to keep the Service reliable and secure.

We do not ask for or store payment card numbers, bank credentials, or other sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service and its features.
  • Display your expenses, summaries, and spending patterns back to you.
  • Authenticate your account and protect against unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical issues and monitor the health of the Service.
  • Respond to your support requests and communicate important changes.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in databases used to run the Service and is retained for as long as your account remains active. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

You may request deletion of your account at any time. When you do, your account and associated expense data will be removed from active systems within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records by law.

4. Third Party Services

We rely on a small number of third-party providers to run the Service — for example, hosting and infrastructure providers, and error-monitoring tools. These providers process data only on our instructions and only to the extent needed to perform their function.

The Service does not embed third-party advertising or social media tracking. If we add any integration that shares data with another provider, we will update this policy before it takes effect.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the support channels listed within the application. We will respond as promptly as we reasonably can.
